Launch to Readout in One Day: Running a Concept Test with AI

I launched a three-variant concept test, analyzed the results, and built a 12-slide readout on the same day. The speed came from checking the survey carefully before launch.

Before launch

I reviewed the whole survey end to end: screener logic, stimuli, and whether the survey matched the research brief. I also drafted and saved the stakeholder preview message. The review caught one problem. A survey-tool integration had been added to the wrong team channel during setup, and I removed it before it caused noise.

After results came in

  • Ran quantitative and qualitative analysis across all three variants.
  • Synthesized open-ended responses into themes that cut across the variants.
  • Wrote a recommendations document that shows where every claim comes from.
  • Built the full 12-slide readout deck, ready to share the same day.

What the study found

The gating question was whether people could understand a set of new icons. One option passed. The other two had distinguishability problems that would need fixing before either could ship. The open responses also pointed to three specific words that caused confusion across all three options, so the design team got a precise fix instead of a vague direction.

Takeaway

AI made the analysis and reporting fast. The pre-launch review is what made the speed safe. A same-day turnaround only helps if the clean answer is also the correct one.
