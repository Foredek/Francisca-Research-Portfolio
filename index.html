Talks

Each talk draws on real studies, with the client details removed, and can be shortened or extended to fit the slot.

Work Smarter, Research Better: Using AI Across the Research Workflow

How I built an AI research engine from one custom skill to 22, what it did on real studies, where it failed, and the calibration habit that made it better.

  • Which parts of the research workflow AI speeds up, with real time savings
  • How to turn every correction into a rule the tool follows next time
  • A starter question to ask at the end of every working session

Trust the Evidence: Quality Checks for AI-Assisted Research

Two automated checks, quote fidelity and data accuracy, caught errors before a stakeholder readout. A practical walkthrough of checks every team can add.

  • How to verify quotes and counts before they reach a slide
  • Where AI summaries drift, and how to catch it
  • How to protect stakeholder trust under deadline pressure

Three Rounds, One Pivot: Designing Research Programs That Change Product Decisions

A case study of a solo, three-round program that caught a failing design direction before launch, and why program design matters more than any single method.

  • How to give each round a different job
  • Why reading every open-ended answer can reverse a headline result
  • How to push back on a one-shot test without losing the room

Small Samples, Strong Claims: Moderated Research You Can Defend

How to get a defensible readout from seven sessions with pre-set thresholds, honest counts, and triangulation.

  • Evidence labels to set before fieldwork
  • How to triangulate against larger or earlier studies
  • How to reframe a readout when the roadmap moves

Speaker bio

Francisca Oredeko is a customer insights and research manager with more than ten years of experience in healthcare, retail, consulting, and consumer apps. She holds an MS.Ed in Applied Psychology from the University of Pennsylvania and a BBA in Marketing and Strategy from Emory University. She has led more than 50 mixed-method projects, co-authored peer-reviewed clinical research, and writes about using AI in real product, market, UX, and CX research. She is based in Atlanta, Georgia.
