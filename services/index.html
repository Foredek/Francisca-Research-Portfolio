What I can run for you

Customer insights and market research

Who your customers are, what they need, and how they choose. I run segmentation, persona work, competitive research, and brand tracking, and I tie the evidence to brand, product, and business decisions.

You get: Segments and personas, competitive landscape, brand tracking read, strategy recommendations

UX and product research

Usability testing, prototype validation, discovery interviews, diary studies, and A/B and preference tests, matched to the decision your team has to make.

You get: Findings by priority, severity-rated issues, design recommendations, a follow-up study scope

CX research

Journey mapping, feedback loops, NPS and CSAT programs, and UX benchmarking, so customer input keeps reaching the people who build the product.

You get: Journey maps, benchmark scorecards, a feedback loop your team can run

Surveys and concept testing

Questionnaire and screener design, stimuli and concept tests, preference tests, and analysis. I have taken a three-variant concept test from launch to a 12-slide readout in one day.

You get: Survey instrument, screener, analysis, readout deck with recommendations tied to evidence

Healthcare research

Research with clinicians, patients, and the teams that serve them, from implementation studies to digital health products. Peer-reviewed publications in palliative care, critical care, and digital health.

You get: Study design, clinician and patient findings, implementation insights

AI-assisted research

AI-moderated interviews, and AI workflows for screeners, synthesis, and quality checks, built with the review steps that keep the evidence trustworthy.

You get: Faster fieldwork and reporting, with quote and data checks before every readout

What you get on every project

  • A research plan with the decision, objectives, method, sample, and timeline
  • Screener and recruiting criteria
  • Discussion guide or survey instrument
  • Fieldwork and moderation, in person or remote
  • Coded analysis that compares themes across methods
  • A readout deck or document with recommendations tied to evidence
  • A scoped follow-up study, so the next decision has a starting point

How a project works

01

Start with the decision

We name the decision your team has to make and the evidence it lacks. Objectives follow from that.

02

Scope the method

I choose the method that answers the question in the time you have, and I tell you what it will not answer.

03

Field the study

I write the screener and guide or survey, recruit, and run the sessions myself.

04

Synthesize

I code every response, compare themes across sources, and check every quote and count before it reaches you.

05

Read out and plan the next study

You get recommendations your team can act on and a scoped next step.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of research projects do you take on?

Customer insights, market, UX, CX, and healthcare research. That includes surveys, concept tests, moderated and AI-moderated interviews, usability tests, diary studies, A/B and preference tests, journey mapping, and benchmarking. Projects range from a single study to a multi-round research program.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the method and the sample. For reference, a seven-session prototype validation took about two weeks in field and synthesis, and a three-round research program with 209 participants took about six weeks. A survey-based concept test can go from launch to readout in a day when the survey is reviewed carefully first.

Do you work remotely?

I am based in Atlanta, Georgia. I run studies remotely and in person, and I am open to work with teams anywhere.

How do you price a project?

I quote each project after a short scoping conversation, based on the decision, the method, and the sample.

Do you use AI in your research?

Yes, with review steps built in. I use AI-moderated interviews where the questions are known, and AI workflows for screeners, synthesis, and reporting. Every quote and number is checked against the source before a readout. I write about this on the AI Research Lab blog.

What industries have you worked in?

Consumer apps, retail and e-commerce, healthcare, and consulting and finance.

Have a project in mind?

Send me the decision you need to make, who your customers or users are, and when you need answers. I will reply with questions and a proposed approach.
